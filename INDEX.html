TRAIN SMART

WORKOUT PLANS

Structured workouts for different fitness goals.

Beginner Workout

A simple full-body routine for people starting their fitness journey.

  1. Bodyweight Squats — 3 × 12
  2. Push Ups — 3 × 8–12
  3. Lunges — 3 × 10
  4. Plank — 3 × 30 sec
  5. Jumping Jacks — 3 × 20

Push Workout

Train your chest, shoulders and triceps.

  1. Bench Press — 4 × 8–12
  2. Shoulder Press — 3 × 8–12
  3. Incline Press — 3 × 10
  4. Lateral Raises — 3 × 12–15
  5. Tricep Pushdown — 3 × 12

Pull Workout

Train your back and biceps with a structured pull session.

  1. Lat Pulldown — 4 × 10
  2. Seated Row — 3 × 10
  3. Face Pull — 3 × 15
  4. Bicep Curl — 3 × 12
  5. Hammer Curl — 3 × 12

Leg Workout

Build stronger legs using compound and isolation movements.

  1. Squats — 4 × 8–10
  2. Leg Press — 3 × 10
  3. Romanian Deadlift — 3 × 10
  4. Leg Curl — 3 × 12
  5. Calf Raises — 4 × 15

NUTRITION GUIDE

FUEL YOUR BODY

Understand the basics of nutrition and how nutrients support your body.

Protein

Protein supports muscle repair and growth. Sources include eggs, chicken, fish, dairy and legumes.

Carbohydrates

Carbohydrates provide energy for daily activities and exercise.

Healthy Fats

Dietary fats support normal body functions. Nuts, seeds and fish are common sources.

Hydration

Water is essential for normal body functions and performance.

Calories

Calories are a measure of energy provided by food and drinks.

Recovery

Good nutrition, sleep and recovery can support your fitness progress.
